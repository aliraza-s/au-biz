About Polar Bear Pet Grooming

A friendly grooming salon in the heart of Ballarat.

Polar Bear Pet Grooming has been grooming Ballarat's dogs for around 11 years, from our salon on Peel Street South in Bakery Hill.

Katie and the team look after everything from quick brush-outs and nail trims to full breed clips and first puppy grooms.

We also stock accessories, collars, leads and food, so you can pick up what you need when you collect your freshly groomed pet.
