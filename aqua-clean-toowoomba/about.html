About Aqua-Clean

Family-owned cleaning, Toowoomba QLD.

Aqua-Clean is a small, family-owned local business that has been servicing Toowoomba and surrounds since 2002.

Sam and Ben specialise in carpet and upholstery steam cleaning, and also clean leather, tiles and grout, outdoor areas and offices. We're a WoolSafe Approved Service Provider and WoolSafe Approved Fibre Care Specialist.

We aim to be punctual, professional, reliable and friendly, with value-for-money pricing. Call 0417 750 466 for a free quote.
