All services & FAQs

What areas do you cover?

Geelong, the Bellarine and the Surf Coast. We're based in Jan Juc.

Can you remove a tree with no machine access?

Yes. We specialise in difficult removals: trees over houses, near delicate gardens and in backyards you can't get a machine into.

Do you do tree reports?

Yes. We prepare tree reports and inventories for schools, caravan parks and development planning, and do expert witness work.

What about emergencies?

We generally work a standard tradesman's day, but can be reached at most hours and will help in emergencies. Call 0400 945 891.
