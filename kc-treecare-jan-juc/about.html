About KC Treecare

Local arborists, Jan Juc.

KC Treecare is run by director Kevin Chisholm, who lives and works in Jan Juc. The business was established in 2009, and the team has 15 years (and counting) as climbing arborists.

Kevin holds a Bachelor of Environmental Science and a Graduate Certificate in Arboriculture from the University of Melbourne (Burnley campus). He employs local people, and the team of six full-time arborists covers Geelong, the Bellarine and the Surf Coast.
