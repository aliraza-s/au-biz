About Busselton Spa & Pool

Family owned, in Busselton.

Busselton Spa & Pool is a family owned pool and spa business with over 45 years of combined industry experience.

From our warehouse, showroom and retail shop on Ponsford Chase we service, repair and renovate pools and spas, install new equipment, test water and supply chemicals and spare parts.

We're SPASA members, have received SPASA WA Awards of Excellence, and are an authorised agent for Hydromax Spas, Sapphire Spas and Freedom Pools.
