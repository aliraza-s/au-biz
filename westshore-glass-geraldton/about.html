About Westshore Glass

Geraldton glaziers since 2013.

Westshore Glass was founded by Geraldton glaziers Adam Wren and Blake Giltrow in March 2013.

Both trained at a Geraldton building supplies business, where Adam ran the glazing section for six years and took Blake on as his apprentice.

Today Westshore Glass is a family owned business providing glazing for homes and businesses across the Mid West and Gascoyne, from a showroom on Box Street.
