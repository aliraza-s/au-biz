About Secura Fence & Fabrication

Family-run fencing and fabrication, Hackham SA.

Peter Jagminas started out on his own as a steel fabricator, estimator and installer, trading as Freebird Fabrications from 2000. Secura Fence & Fabrication began in 2011.

Today it's a family business run by a husband and wife team, with 11 employees. Manufacturing, supply, installation and powder coating are all done in-house in Hackham.

Clients include the Department of Education, local councils, industrial and commercial sites, retirement villages and homeowners right across Adelaide.
