About Ballarat Tree Fella

Tree services from Delacombe, Ballarat.

Ballarat Tree Fella Pty Ltd has been doing tree work for over 25 years, from its office and depot in Delacombe and a second depot at Clarendon.

We look after homeowners as well as Local and State Government departments and the civil construction industry.

Our highly qualified, trained and experienced staff handle everything from formative pruning and consultations to full removals, power line trimming, site clearing and storm clean-ups.
