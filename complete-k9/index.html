All services & FAQs

How do I book?

Call or text Mark on 0432 840 257, or send the booking form and we'll confirm a time with you.

Can my dog do daycare and a groom on the same day?

Yes. Drop them off in the morning for daycare and they'll be groomed before pick-up.

Do you groom senior or anxious dogs?

Yes. We take things slowly and give older and nervous dogs extra time and breaks.

Are you the same business as Buninyong Dog Grooming?

Yes. Mark ran Buninyong Dog Grooming before moving to the Complete K9 salon in Delacombe.
