About Complete K9

Formerly Buninyong Dog Grooming, now a full grooming and daycare salon in Delacombe.

Mark started grooming in Buninyong and built up a loyal client list of dogs and owners who wouldn't go anywhere else.

Complete K9 brings grooming and daycare together in one clean, purpose-set-up space in Delacombe, so your dog can play all day and come home looking great.

The team takes the time to get to know each dog, which is why so many clients have been coming back for years.
