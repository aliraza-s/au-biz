Common questions

Do you really come to my house?

Yes. Our van is a fully equipped mobile salon, so we park outside and groom your dog right there.

Which days can you come?

We work seven days by appointment. Send a request and we'll offer you the next available times.

My dog is anxious. Can you still groom them?

Yes. Many of our regulars are nervous dogs who do much better at home with one groomer and no other dogs around.

How long does a groom take?

Most washes take under an hour; full grooms take longer depending on the coat.
