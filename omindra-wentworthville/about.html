About Omindra

Education and migration consultancy, Wentworthville.

Omindra Consultancy Services is an education and migration consultancy with its Australian office at 76-80 Station Street, Wentworthville, and offices in Kurukshetra and Dehradun in India.

Omindra offers expert solutions for overseas study and immigration, with a focus on Australia and New Zealand. Its mission is to be the most trusted education and migration consultant in India and abroad.

Omindra wants to be the biggest plus factor in students' lives when they go abroad for education or migration.
