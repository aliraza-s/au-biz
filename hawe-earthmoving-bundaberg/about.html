About Hawe Earthmoving

Civil contractors, Bundaberg since 1968.

Hawe Earthmoving has been a civil contracting firm in Bundaberg since 1968. It started with a single dozer and has grown on the back of quality service.

Today the family-owned business takes on civil, commercial and rural projects of all sizes across Bundaberg and the Wide Bay Burnett, from road construction and subdivisions to dams, drainage and concrete works, with its own fleet of plant for hire.
