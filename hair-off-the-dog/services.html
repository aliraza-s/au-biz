Double coat care

Double coats need the undercoat lifted out properly, not shaved. Natalie de-sheds and brushes out thoroughly, then neatens the outline for a natural, tidy finish.

  • Undercoat removal
  • No unnecessary shaving
  • Neat, natural outline

Common questions

Do I need an appointment?

Yes. The boutique works by appointment so every dog gets Natalie's full attention.

What breeds do you specialise in?

Natalie is especially known for double coats and poodle-type coats, but she grooms all breeds.

How often should my dog be groomed?

Poodle-type coats usually need styling every 4 to 6 weeks; double coats benefit from a de-shed each season.

What are your hours?

Monday 9:15am–4pm, Tuesday to Thursday 8:15am–4pm and Friday 8:15am–1pm.
