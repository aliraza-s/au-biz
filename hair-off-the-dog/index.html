Meet Natalie

Hair Off The Dog is a small, calm boutique where one experienced stylist looks after your dog from bath to final scissor work. No production line, no rushing.

Some clients have been bringing their dogs to Natalie for six years. She talks to each dog the whole way through, which is why nervous dogs settle with her.

Coats she's known for

Double coats

Thorough de-shedding and brush-outs with a neat, natural finish that looks after the coat.

Poodle-type coats

Poodles, oodles and curly crosses. Careful scissor work and maintenance clips that keep matting away between visits.

Full styling

Bath, blow-dry, clip and hand-scissored finish.

Nail trims, ear cleaning and de-matting.
