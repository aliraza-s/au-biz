About IMC

International Migration Centre, Wentworthville, since 2005.

International Migration Centre (IMC) was founded in 2005 and is an Australian owned and operated business, upstairs at 57-59 Dunmore Street, Wentworthville.

IMC has helped thousands of overseas individuals, families and local corporate clients with their migration matters, including 1,225 student visas, 556 family visas and 450 travel visas.

Clients are kept up to date at every stage, and IMC can arrange an interpreter for your language if you need one.
