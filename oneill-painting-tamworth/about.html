About O'Neill Painting Services

Dulux Accredited Painter, North Tamworth NSW.

O'Neill Painting Services is run by Nicholas (Nick) O'Neill, who has more than 20 years experience in the painting industry.

The business began in Coonabarabran and has grown into Gunnedah, Tamworth and Armidale. As a member of the Dulux Accredited Painters program, jobs come with a 5-year workmanship warranty.

From a few rooms at home to schools, service stations and epoxy floors, call Nick on 0429 919 116 to talk about the job.
