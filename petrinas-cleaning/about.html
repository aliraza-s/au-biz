Meet Petrina

A local cleaning business built on reliability and kindness.

Petrina's Cleaning Services is a small local team based in Scarborough, looking after homes and units across the Redcliffe Peninsula.

Petrina handles the bookings and keeps in touch with every client, while her cleaners, including Kim, Kristy and Dot, take care of the cleaning.

Clients say it's the communication that sets the business apart: reminders before each clean, quick replies and a genuine check that you're happy.
