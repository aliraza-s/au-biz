Common questions

Do you give free quotes?

Yes. Quotes and site inspections are free, and we prefer to meet you on site.

How soon can you start?

Usually about 1 to 2 weeks, depending on our work schedule.

Can you remove old concrete first?

Yes. We have a Bobcat and truck to remove soil, old concrete and pavers.

What areas do you cover?

Mainly Toowoomba and surrounds, and we have worked much further afield across Queensland.

How can I pay?

EFT, cheque, money order or cash.
