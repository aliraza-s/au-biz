About Schwerin Concreting

A Toowoomba family business, built one slab at a time.

Schwerin Concreting is a locally owned family business based in Rangeville, Toowoomba.

The family has been concreting since 1969, when John's brother Neil started in the trade and passed it on to John. John now runs the business with his sons Jonson and Jake.

We take on residential, commercial and industrial work, from driveways and paths to shed and factory slabs, and we're QBCC licensed.
