Meet Al

Mobile dog groomer, Ballarat East.

Al's Dog-Wash Ballarat is run by Al (Alex), from Ballarat East.

He specialises in golden retrievers and border collies, plus large dogs with long or double coats: the breeds that take the most time and patience to groom.

The service is mobile, so your dog stays home. Bookings are online 24/7, or call Al on 0400 508 057.
