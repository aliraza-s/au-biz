Meet Al

A mobile groomer who treats every dog like his own.

Al's Dog-Wash started with a simple idea: dogs are calmer at home, so bring the wash to them.

Al works one dog at a time from a fully fitted van, with warm water, professional dryers and grooming gear on board. That means no car trips, no cages and no waiting around in a noisy salon.

He's built a loyal following across Ballarat, especially with owners of big, fluffy and nervous dogs who have struggled with groomers before.
