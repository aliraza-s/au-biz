Common questions

Do I need to be home?

It's best if someone is home to hand your dog over and pay, but Al can work around you. Just mention it when you book.

What does the van need from me?

Nothing. The van carries its own warm water and power, so Al only needs somewhere to park near your driveway.

Can you handle anxious or reactive dogs?

Yes. Al takes time to settle each dog first, and many clients book him because their dog does better at home than in a salon.

How often should my dog be washed?

Most dogs do well every 4 to 8 weeks. Double-coated breeds benefit from a de-shed at the change of seasons.
