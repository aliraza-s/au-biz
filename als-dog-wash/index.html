What Al does

Every visit is one dog at a time, at your place, with warm water and gear set up in the van.

Wash and blow-dry

Hydrobath with a gentle shampoo, then a thorough dry so the coat comes up fluffy and smells fresh for days.

De-shed and brush-out

For huskies, spitz, collies and other double-coated breeds. Undercoat out, no knots left behind.

Groom and tidy

Face, feet and hygiene tidy-ups, plus full clips. Nails trimmed on every visit if you'd like.

Good with the dogs other groomers find hard

Big dogs, old dogs and worried dogs do better at home. There's no car trip, no kennel and no barking room, just Al, the van and your driveway.
