About AVEC

Australian Visa & Education Consultants, Granville.

Australian Visa & Education Consultants (AVEC) helps people with visas, migration and education in Australia.

Australian immigration law is complex and changes often. Our registered migration agents follow those changes so your application is prepared correctly, and we represent clients before the Department of Home Affairs and in merits review.

We also represent Australian colleges and universities for students who want to study here. Fees are low and transparent, and payment plans are available.
