About Express Auto Electrics

Auto electrical and air conditioning, Callemondah QLD.

Express Auto Electrics handles auto electrical and auto air conditioning work for Gladstone and the surrounding areas, from a workshop at Unit 1/12 Bassett Street, Callemondah.

We work on cars, 4WDs, trucks, earthmoving equipment and plant, either in the workshop or mobile on site. Air conditioning work is licensed and certified by the Australian Refrigeration Council.

From a flat battery to a full light bar and dual-battery fit-out, call (07) 4972 7211 to book.
