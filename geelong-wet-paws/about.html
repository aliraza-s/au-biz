About Luke

Geelong's calm, down-to-earth mobile groomer.

Geelong Wet Paws is a one-man mobile grooming service run by Luke, based at Bell Post Hill.

Because Luke does every groom himself, your dog sees the same face each time, which makes a big difference for nervous dogs, seniors and puppies.

The fully equipped van means no car trips and no waiting in a busy salon. Just a calm groom, right outside your door.
