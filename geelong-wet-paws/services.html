What Luke does

Everything happens in the van outside your place, one dog at a time.

Wash & dry

A warm hydrobath with shampoo to suit the coat, then a full blow-dry and brush-out. Big double coats like goldens and shepherds come up soft and shed far less afterwards.

  • Shampoo to suit the coat
  • Full blow-dry and brush-out
  • Great for big double coats

Full groom

A complete groom: wash, dry, clip or scissor cut, plus a tidy of the face, feet and hygiene areas and an ear clean. Luke is persistent with coats that other groomers have struggled with.

  • Clip or scissor finish
  • Face, feet and hygiene tidy
  • Ears cleaned

Nail trims

Calm, careful nail trims that owners say their dogs actually tolerate. Add it to any wash, or book it on its own.

  • Slow, calm handling
  • Add to any service
  • Senior dogs welcome

Puppy first groom

Common questions

Where do you travel to?

Geelong and surrounding suburbs. Let us know your suburb when you book and we'll confirm.

What days do you work?

Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm.

Do I need to provide water or power?

No. The van is fully self-contained.

Can you groom an older or grumpy dog?

Yes. Luke takes his time and many of his regulars are seniors.
