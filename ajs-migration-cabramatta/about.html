About AJS

Registered migration agent Jenny Tew, Cabramatta.

AJS Education & Migration Agent is run by Jenny Tew, a registered migration agent (MARN 0214733) at Level 1, Suite 12, 49 Park Road, Cabramatta.

Jenny lived through the Pol Pot regime, so she understands how painful it is to be separated from family. For years she has helped clients with student, spouse and visitor visas, as well as family, skilled and business visas.

She holds immigration law qualifications from the University of New South Wales and has worked as an interpreter in courts, tribunals, medical settings and with the UNHCR. She speaks Khmer, Mandarin, Teochew, Cantonese and Vietnamese.
