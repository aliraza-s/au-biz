About Melton Migration

Migration agents and lawyers, Melton South.

Melton Migration is a migration consultancy at 14 Harper Street, Melton South, led by registered migration agent Mohammad Uddin (MARN 2518732).

The team includes registered migration agents and lawyers, and takes on complex and high-risk matters as well as everyday visas: visa refusals, cancellations, tribunal reviews and Federal Court appeals.

Whether you are planning to migrate or to study in Australia or overseas, the team builds a strategy around your case. Their policy is simple: check twice, submit once.
