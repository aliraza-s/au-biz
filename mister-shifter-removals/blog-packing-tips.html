Packing tips that make the truck load faster (and cost less)

October 2026 · Mister Shifter Removals

The crew can only move as fast as the house is packed. A well-packed house loads in a fraction of the time, and that shows up on your bill.

Use proper boxes

Uniform boxes stack. Random bags, laundry baskets and open containers don't, and they take up more truck space than they should. Book boxes for books and heavy things, tea chests for linen and light items.

Heavy on the bottom, light on top

Pack heavy items in small boxes so they're still liftable. Fill gaps with paper or towels so nothing moves. Tape the bottom twice.

Label the room, not the contents

'Kitchen' on two sides of the box is more useful than a list of what's inside. The crew puts it where it belongs without asking, and you unpack room by room.

Disassemble what you can

Bed frames, dining tables and flat-pack furniture travel better in pieces. Keep screws and bolts in a labelled zip bag taped to the piece they belong to.

Leave the fridge, washer and big furniture to us

We wrap and move them. Just have the fridge defrosted and the washing machine drained and disconnected the night before.
