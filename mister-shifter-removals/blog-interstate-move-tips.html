Moving interstate from Lismore: what to sort out first

October 2026 · Mister Shifter Removals

An interstate move is a bigger job than a local one, but most of the extra work is admin. Get these sorted early and the move itself is the easy part.

Get your dates straight

Interstate moves involve a long drive and sometimes an overnight stop, so the pickup day and the delivery day may not be the same. Book early, confirm both dates, and make sure the new place is ready to receive the truck.

Declutter before you pay to move it

Every item on the truck is weight and space you're paying for. Sell, donate or dump anything you haven't used in a year. A green and domestic waste run before the move is often cheaper than carting junk across the border.

Change over the paperwork

Driver's licence and car registration need transferring to the new state within a set period. Update your address with the ATO, Medicare, your super fund, your bank and your employer. Transfer or cancel utilities and internet.

Pack for the highway

Wrap fragile items more than you would for a local move. Fill boxes so nothing rattles. Label the boxes you'll need first and ask for them to go on last so they come off first.

Keep the essentials with you

Passports, medications, important documents, jewellery and anything you can't replace travel in your car, not the truck.
