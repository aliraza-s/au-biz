FAQ

Local moves questions.

How far ahead should I book?

As early as you can, especially for end-of-month and weekend moves. Call and we'll tell you what's open.

Do I need to pack everything?

Boxes packed and sealed before we arrive keeps the move quick. Furniture we'll wrap and handle on the day.

How do I get a quote?

Call or text Will on 0475 074 906, message the Facebook page, or send the form on the contact page. Tell us where from, where to, how big and when.

Do I need to be there on the day?

Someone needs to let the crew in, point out what's going and lock up after. It doesn't have to be you, but it helps.
