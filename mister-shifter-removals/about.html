Our story

Built in Lismore, one move at a time.

Mister Shifter Removals is run by Will out of Lismore. The business started small and has grown with the Northern Rivers: these days it runs pantech trucks and a crew, handling full house and business moves, interstate runs, pickups and deliveries, and domestic and green waste removal.

What hasn't changed is how the job is done. Will quotes it straight, the crew turns up when they said they would, and your stuff gets wrapped, strapped and treated like it's their own.

  • Owner-operated, you deal with Will directly
  • Pantech trucks with blankets, straps and trolleys
  • Local, regional and interstate
