Moving day in the Northern Rivers: a checklist that actually helps

October 2026 · Mister Shifter Removals

A move goes smoothly when the boring bits are done before the truck turns up. Here's the list we'd give our own family.

Two weeks out

Book the truck and lock in the date. Start using up frozen food. Order boxes, tape and a couple of markers. Tell your landlord or agent your dates. Book a cleaner for the old place if you're renting.

One week out

Pack the rooms you use least first: spare room, garage, bookshelves. Label every box with the room it's going to, not just what's in it. Redirect your mail and update your address with the bank, Medicare, the electoral roll and your car rego.

The day before

Defrost the fridge and freezer. Pack a 'first night' box: kettle, mugs, chargers, toiletries, bed linen, a change of clothes and the kids' favourites. Take photos of how the TV and modem cables are plugged in.

Moving day

Clear a path from the front door to the driveway. Keep pets and kids somewhere safe. Walk the crew through the house so they know what's going and what's staying. Do a last check of cupboards, the garden shed and behind doors before you leave.

At the new place

Point the crew to the right rooms as the boxes come off. Beds and the fridge first, everything else can wait. Then put the kettle on.
