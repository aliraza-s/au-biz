FAQ

Answers to the questions we get asked most. Anything else, call Will.

Do you still do man-and-ute moves?

No. Mister Shifter runs pantech trucks with a crew for moves of all sizes, plus pickups, deliveries and waste runs.

What areas do you cover?

Lismore and the Northern Rivers for local work, and interstate moves out of the region. See the service areas page or call to check your suburb.

How do I get a quote?

Call or text Will on 0475 074 906, message the Facebook page, or send the form on the contact page. Tell us where from, where to, how big and when.

Do I need to be there on the day?

Someone needs to let the crew in, point out what's going and lock up after. It doesn't have to be you, but it helps.

Can you move a single item?

Yes. Pickups and deliveries of furniture, whitegoods and marketplace buys are a big part of what we do.

Do you take rubbish as well?

Yes. Domestic and green waste removal, on its own or combined with a move.

How far ahead should I book?

As early as you can, especially for end-of-month and weekend moves. Call and we'll tell you what's open.

Do I need to pack everything?

Boxes packed and sealed before we arrive keeps the move quick. Furniture we'll wrap and handle on the day.

Which states do you go to?

Call with your destination and Will will tell you if it's a run we do.

Is my stuff on the truck with other people's?

Ask when you book. Will can tell you how your move will be loaded.

Can you pick up from a store for me?

Yes. Give us the store, the order details and where it's going.

Do you take things upstairs?

Usually yes. Mention stairs or tight access when you book so we send the right crew.

What can't you take?

Call to check. Some materials have to go to specific facilities.

Can you do it on the same day as my move?

Often yes. Mention it when you book and we'll plan the truck around it.

Got a move coming up?

Call or text Will for a straight quote.
