About Burrows Concreting

A family-run concreting business in Tannum Sands.

JC & WA Burrows Concreting is a family-run concreting business based in Tannum Sands, serving the local area for more than 30 years.

We specialise in residential concreting, from driveways and footpaths to block walls and decorative finishes, and take on commercial jobs as well.

Our gear covers it all: mixers and finishing tools, compact excavation and formwork equipment, and tools for decorative and textured finishes.
